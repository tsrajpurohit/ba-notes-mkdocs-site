    Resume Optimization + LinkedIn Profile Building + Job Application Strategy

    Objective

    Convert your learning, projects, and skills into a market-ready profile for roles such as:

    • Business Analyst

    • Healthcare Business Analyst

    • Product Analyst

    • AI Product Analyst

    • Associate Product Manager

    • AI Product Manager (entry/transition level)

    Your positioning will be:

    Pharmacology Background

    +

    Healthcare Domain Knowledge

    +

    Business Analysis Skills

    +

    Agile/Jira Skills

    +

    AI Product Understanding

    ↓

    Healthcare AI Business Analyst / Product Analyst

    PHASE 1 -- Resume Optimization

    Lesson 1 -- Resume Strategy for Career Transition

    Before writing a resume, we need to solve the biggest challenge:

    \"How do we position a non-IT background?\"

    Your resume should NOT look like:

    ❌ Pharmacologist trying to enter IT

    It should look like:

    ✅ Healthcare domain professional who transitioned into Healthcare Technology Business Analysis

    1. Resume Positioning Strategy

    Traditional Resume:

    Education

    Work Experience

    Skills

    Projects

    For your profile, we use:

    Professional Summary

    Core BA Skills

    Healthcare Technology Projects

    Business Analysis Experience

    Agile/Jira Skills

    Domain Expertise

    Education

    2. Target Resume Title

    Avoid:

    ❌ Fresher Business Analyst

    Because you are not starting from zero.

    Better titles:

    Option 1:

    Healthcare Business Analyst | AI Product Analyst | Agile BA

    Option 2:

    Business Analyst -- Healthcare Technology | AI Solutions | Product Analytics

    Option 3:

    Healthcare Domain Analyst transitioning into Business Analysis & AI Product Management

    3. Professional Summary Structure

    A strong summary should answer:

    Who are you?

    Healthcare professional with pharmacology background.

    What skills do you have?

    • Business analysis

    • Requirement gathering

    • Agile delivery

    • Jira

    • Process modelling

    • Product thinking

    What domain?

    Healthcare + AI.

    What value?

    Bridge between healthcare stakeholders and technology teams.

    Example structure:

    Healthcare domain professional with a background in pharmacology,

    transitioning into Business Analysis and AI-driven healthcare solutions.

    Experienced in requirement analysis, Agile delivery practices,

    process modelling, Jira-based backlog management, and healthcare

    product concepts.

    Built portfolio projects covering healthcare platforms, AI solutions,

    requirements documentation, user stories, BPMN workflows, and UAT

    processes.

    Strong ability to translate clinical/business needs into technology

    solutions.

    1. How Recruiters Review Resumes

    A recruiter usually spends:

    6--10 seconds on the first scan

    They look for:

    1. Target role match

    2. Relevant keywords

    3. Recent experience

    4. Projects

    5. Tools

    6. Domain knowledge

    2. ATS (Applicant Tracking System)

    Many companies do not manually review every resume.

    ATS scans for keywords.

    Example job description:

    Business Analyst with experience in Agile, Jira, requirements gathering, user stories, BRD, UAT, stakeholder management.

    ATS looks for:

    Business Analyst

    Agile

    Jira

    Requirements Gathering

    User Stories

    BRD

    UAT

    Stakeholder Management

    Your resume must naturally include these terms.

    Lesson 5 -- ATS Optimization & Resume Tailoring Strategy

    1. What is ATS?

    ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is software companies use to filter resumes before recruiters see them.

    It checks:

    • Job title match

    • Skills keywords

    • Experience relevance

    • Tools

    • Domain knowledge

    2. The Biggest Mistake

    Many candidates use one generic resume everywhere.

    Example:

    Applying for:

    Healthcare Business Analyst

    but resume says mostly:

    • Pharmacology

    • Research

    • Laboratory skills

    ATS may reject because BA keywords are missing.

    3. Create Multiple Resume Versions

    For your profile, create 3 versions:

    Version 1: Healthcare Business Analyst Resume ⭐ (Primary)

    Target:

    • Healthcare BA

    • Clinical BA

    • Healthcare IT Analyst

    Focus keywords:

    • Requirements Gathering

    • BRD

    • FRD

    • Stakeholder Management

    • BPMN

    • UAT

    4. Common BA Questions

    Q1. What does a Business Analyst do?

    Answer:

    A Business Analyst identifies business problems, understands stakeholder needs, analyzes processes, documents requirements, and works with technology teams to deliver solutions that achieve business objectives.

    Q2. Difference between BRD and FRD?

    BRD FRD

    Business need System behavior

    Why build? What should system do?

    Business stakeholders Business + technical teams

    Q3. How do you gather requirements?

    Answer:

    Methods:

    • Interviews

    • Workshops

    • Observation

    • Document analysis

    • Surveys

    • Prototyping

    Q4. How do you handle changing requirements?

    Answer:

    First understand the reason for change, perform impact analysis, evaluate business value, update documentation, communicate with stakeholders, and manage approval before implementation.

    5. Agile Questions

    Q. What is a User Story?

    Answer:

    A user story describes a requirement from the user\'s perspective, usually written as: As a [user], I want [goal], so that [benefit].

    Example:

    As a patient, I want to book appointments online so that I can avoid waiting time.

    Q. What is BA role in Scrum?

    BA helps with:

    • Requirement clarification

    • Backlog refinement

    • User story creation

    • Acceptance criteria

    • Stakeholder communication

    • UAT support

    6. Jira Questions

    Q. How have you used Jira?

    Answer:

    I have used Jira to structure product backlog items using epics, features, and user stories. I have worked with workflows, sprint planning, issue tracking, JQL filters, dashboards, and Agile reporting concepts.
