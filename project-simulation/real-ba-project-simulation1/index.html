    1. Business Problem vs Business Case

    Many beginners confuse these.

    Business Problem

    Answers:

    \"What is wrong today?\"

    Example:

    Answers:

    \"Why should the organization invest money to solve this problem?\"

    • Current cost

    • Expected benefits

    • Investment required

    • Business value

    • Risks of not acting
