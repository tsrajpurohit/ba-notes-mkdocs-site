    A BA does not stop at symptoms.

    Problem:

    Trial delays occur frequently.

    Why are issues identified late?

    Why 3:

    Why is monitoring manual?

    → No predictive analytics system exists.

    Lack of centralized real-time monitoring and risk prediction capability.

    Lesson 17 -- AI Clinical Trial Management Platform

    Data Requirements, AI Features & Product Analytics

    Understanding BA Role in AI Healthcare Products

    Learning Objective

    After this lesson, you will understand:

    ✅ Why AI projects need different BA analysis\ ✅ How to identify data requirements\ ✅ How to define AI features from business problems\ ✅ How BAs work with Data Scientists and AI Engineers\ ✅ How to define AI product metrics and success measures

    1. Why AI Projects Need Special BA Skills

    Traditional software:

    User Input

    ↓

    Business Rules

    ↓

    System Output

    Example:

    Patient selects appointment slot → System books appointment.

    AI Software:

    Business Problem

    ↓

    Data

    ↓

    AI Model

    ↓

    Prediction / Recommendation

    ↓

    Human Decision

    Example:

    Trial data → AI analyzes risk → Predicts delay → CRA takes action.

    2. BA Role in AI Product Development

    A BA does not build the AI model.

    The BA defines:

    Business Problem

    \"What decision needs improvement?\"

    Data Requirement

    \"What information is needed?\"

    AI Output

    \"What should the system provide?\"

    Business Action

    \"What will users do with the output?\"

    3. AI Feature Analysis Framework

    For every AI feature define:

    Problem

    ↓

    AI Capability

    ↓

    Required Data

    ↓

    Output

    ↓

    User Action

    ↓

    Business Value

    Example: AI Trial Risk Prediction

    Problem

    Clinical teams discover trial delays too late.

    AI Capability

    3. Compliance Requirements for Clinical Trial Platform

    Requirement 1: Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

    Business Need:

    Different users require different access levels.

    Example:

    Role Access

    Sponsor Trial overview

    CRA Site monitoring

    Investigator Patient visit data

    Regulatory Team Documents

    Functional Requirement:

    FR-COMP-001:

    The system shall restrict access based on user roles.

    Requirement 2: Audit Trail

    Business Need:

    Clinical activities must be traceable.

    System should record:

    • Who changed data

    • What changed

    • When changed

    Requirement:

    The system shall maintain an audit history for critical activities.

    Requirement 3: Data Integrity

    Business Need:

    Clinical data must remain accurate.

    System should:

    • Validate inputs

    • Prevent unauthorized changes

    • Maintain history

    Requirement 4: Document Version Control

    Business Need:

    Clinical documents go through multiple revisions.

    System should maintain:

    Document v1

    ↓

    Review

    ↓

    Document v2

    ↓

    Approval
