    7. Root Cause Analysis (RCA)

    Problem:

    Document Review Takes Too Long

    5 Why Analysis

    Why 1:

    Why is review slow?

    Because employees manually read documents.

    Why 2:

    Why manually read?

    Because information extraction is not automated.

    Why 3:

    Why no automation?

    Because documents are unstructured.

    Why 4:

    Why are documents difficult?

    Because they contain text, tables, images, and different formats.

    Why 5:

    Why no intelligent system?

    Because existing systems only store documents, they do not understand content.

    Root Cause:

    Lack of AI-powered document understanding capability.

    Lesson 27 -- TO-BE Process Design, AI Workflow & Future-State BPMN

    Designing the Future Enterprise Process With AI Automation

    Learning Objective

    After this lesson, you will understand:

    ✅ How a BA transforms an inefficient AS-IS process into an optimized TO-BE process\ ✅ How AI changes business workflows\ ✅ How to design Human-in-the-Loop AI processes\ ✅ How to create future-state BPMN thinking\ ✅ How to convert AI capabilities into business requirements

    1. AS-IS vs TO-BE Thinking

    A BA does not simply automate the old process.

    Wrong approach:

    \"Replace manual reading with AI.\"

    Better approach:

    \"Redesign the complete document lifecycle using AI while maintaining human control and compliance.\"
