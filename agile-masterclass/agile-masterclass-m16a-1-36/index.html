    You have joined a pharmaceutical company as a:

    Business Analyst -- Healthcare AI Product Team

    AI Pharmacovigilance Automation Platform

    Business Context

    The company receives thousands of safety reports from:

    • Patients

    • Doctors

    • Hospitals

    • Clinical trials

    • Regulatory authorities

    • Literature monitoring

    Currently, the process is mostly manual.

    Current Problems

    Learning Objective

    After this lesson, you will understand:

    ✅ How a BA conducts stakeholder interviews\ ✅ How to ask the right discovery questions\ ✅ How to identify hidden requirements\ ✅ How to convert conversations into requirements\ ✅ How to document interview findings\ ✅ How to handle different stakeholder perspectives

    1. Why Stakeholder Interviews Matter

    A BA does not get requirements by simply asking:

    \"What features do you need?\"

    That usually produces solutions, not problems.

    Example:

    Stakeholder says:

    \"We need an AI chatbot.\"

    A BA asks:

    \"What problem will the chatbot solve?\"

    The answer may be:

    \"Patients cannot get appointment information quickly.\"

    The real requirement is not the chatbot.

    The real requirement is:

    \"Patients need faster access to appointment information.\"

    1. What is AS-IS Process Modeling?

    AS-IS Process Model represents:

    \"How the business currently works today.\"

    It captures:

    • Current activities

    • People involved

    • Systems used

    • Inputs and outputs

    • Problems and delays

    Why do we create AS-IS before TO-BE?

    Because a BA must understand:

    \"What are we improving?\"

    before answering:

    \"What should we build?\"

    Example

    Business says:

    \"We need AI automation.\"

    A weak BA:

    ❌ Starts writing AI requirements.

    An experienced BA:

    ✅ Studies the current workflow first.
