    Lesson 23 -- Product Prioritization, MVP Strategy & Feature Roadmap

    Using MoSCoW, RICE & WSJF Like a Product Analyst

    Learning Objective

    After this lesson, you will understand:

    ✅ How product teams decide what to build first\ ✅ Difference between MVP and complete product vision\ ✅ How BAs support Product Owners in prioritization\ ✅ How to use MoSCoW, RICE, and WSJF frameworks\ ✅ How to create a realistic feature roadmap

    1. Why Prioritization Is Important

    In real projects:

    You have:

    • 100+ requirements

    • Limited budget

    • Limited developers

    • Fixed timelines

    You cannot build everything together.

    The question becomes:

    \"Which features create the highest business value first?\"

    2. BA Role in Prioritization

    • Is it required for MVP?

    5. RICE Prioritization Framework

    Used by Product Managers.

    RICE:

    R = Reach

    I = Impact

    C = Confidence

    E = Effort

    Score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) / Effort

    Example:

    Feature:

    AI Risk Prediction

    Reach:

    500 users

    Impact:

    3 (High)

    Confidence:

    80%

    Effort:

    10 person-weeks

    Calculation:

    (500 × 3 × 0.8) / 10

    =

    120

    Feature Comparison

    Feature RICE Score

    AI Risk Prediction 120

    Mobile App 40

    Chatbot 25

    Decision:

    Build AI Risk Prediction first.

    6. WSJF Prioritization

    Used heavily in SAFe environments.

    Formula:

    WSJF =

    Cost of Delay

    ÷

    Job Size

    Cost of Delay:

    Includes:

    Business Value

    How valuable?

    Time Criticality

    How urgent?

    Risk Reduction

    How much risk reduced?

    Example:

    Feature:

    Regulatory Audit Trail

    Business Value:

    10

    Time Criticality:

    8

    Risk Reduction:

    10

    Cost of Delay:

    28

    Job Size:

    4

    WSJF:

    7

    Higher WSJF:

    Build earlier.

    2. Difference Between Metric and KPI

    Many beginners confuse these.

    Metric

    A measurement.

    It tells:

    \"What is happening?\"

    Example:

    Number of dashboard visits

    KPI

    A critical business measurement.

    It tells:

    \"Are we achieving our goal?\"

    Example:

    Reduction in trial monitoring time

    Example:

    Measurement Type

    Number of users logged in Metric

    Monthly active users Metric

    Increase in adoption by 50% KPI

    Reduce trial delays by 20% KPI

    2. The STAR + BA Framework

    Use this structure:

    S - Situation

    T - Task

    A - Analysis & Actions

    R - Result

    Example Project:

    AI Clinical Trial Management Platform

    S -- Situation

    Business Context

    \"A pharmaceutical organization was managing clinical trials using multiple disconnected systems. Trial teams relied heavily on spreadsheets and manual reports, resulting in delayed risk identification and limited operational visibility.\"

    T -- Task

    BA Responsibility

    \"My role was to analyze clinical trial workflows, identify process gaps, define requirements, support Agile delivery, and ensure the solution addressed operational challenges.\"

    A -- Analysis & Actions

    This is where you demonstrate BA skills.

    Step 1: Stakeholder Analysis

    Identified:

    Stakeholder Need

    4. Common Interview Questions

    Q1:

    \"How did you gather requirements?\"

    Answer:

    \"I used stakeholder interviews, workshops, process analysis, and document review. I validated requirements through reviews and converted them into user stories with acceptance criteria.\"

    Q2:

    \"How did you handle conflicting requirements?\"

    Answer:

    \"I analyzed stakeholder priorities, understood the underlying business need, evaluated impact and feasibility, and worked with the Product Owner to prioritize requirements.\"

    Q3:

    \"How did you work with developers?\"

    Answer:

    \"I supported backlog refinement, clarified acceptance criteria, answered requirement questions, and ensured delivered features matched business expectations.\"

    Q4:

    \"How did you validate AI features?\"

    Answer:

    \"I focused on AI output accuracy, explainability, user acceptance, and human-in-the-loop workflows. The AI recommendation was validated before business action.\"
