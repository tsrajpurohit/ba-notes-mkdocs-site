    1. What is Confluence?

    Simple Definition

    Confluence is a collaborative documentation and knowledge management platform used by teams to create, store, organize, and share information.

    Think of it as:

    A company\'s digital knowledge library.

    Real-Life Analogy

    Imagine a hospital.

    Different departments maintain documents:

    • Medical procedures

    • Training manuals

    • SOPs

    • Research documents

    • Meeting notes

    • Policies

    A physical library stores everything.

    Confluence is the digital version of that library.

    Technical Definition

    Confluence is a collaboration tool developed by Atlassian.

    It is commonly integrated with Jira.

    2. Why Does Confluence Exist?

    Before tools like Confluence, companies used:

    • Word documents

    • Email attachments

    • Shared folders

    Problems:

    ❌ Multiple versions

    ❌ Lost documents

    ❌ Difficult collaboration

    ❌ No history tracking

    ❌ Poor knowledge sharing

    Confluence solves this by providing:

    ✔ One central location

    ✔ Version history

    ✔ Comments

    ✔ Collaboration

    ✔ Search

    ✔ Templates

    1. What is Jira?

    Simple Definition

    Jira is a work management and issue-tracking tool used to plan, track, and deliver software projects.

    Think of Jira as the central workspace where every requirement, task, bug, sprint, and release is managed.

    2. Why Was Jira Created?

    Imagine building a Hospital Management System.

    The project has:

    • 300 requirements

    • 1,500 user stories

    • 20 developers

    • 8 testers

    • 3 Business Analysts

    • 2 Product Managers

    • Hundreds of bugs

    Managing this in Excel would quickly become unmanageable.

    Jira was created to solve this problem.

    3. Real-Life Analogy

    • Architects

    • What needs to be done.

    • What\'s completed.

    • What\'s blocked.

    7. Jira Terminology

    Term Meaning

    Project A container for all work

    Issue Any work item in Jira

    Epic Large body of work

    Story User requirement

    Task Technical or general work

    3. Company-managed vs Team-managed Projects

    Company-managed Project

    Managed by Jira Administrators.

    Best for:

    • Medium/Large companies.

    • Enterprise environments.

    • Multiple Agile teams.

    • Standardized workflows.

    Characteristics:

    ✔ Common workflows

    ✔ Standard issue types

    ✔ Central permissions

    ✔ Governance

    ✔ Highly scalable

    Team-managed Project

    Managed by the project team itself.

    Best for:

    • Small teams.

    • Startups.

    • Proof of Concepts.

    • MVPs.

    Characteristics:

    ✔ Quick setup

    ✔ Flexible

    ✔ Team controls configuration

    ✔ Minimal administration

    Comparison

    Company-managed Team-managed

    Enterprise Small teams

    Central administration Team controlled

    Complex workflows Simple workflows

    Highly customizable Easier to manage

    Best for large organizations Best for startups

    Which Should You Learn?

    For interviews:

    Know both.

    For enterprise healthcare companies:

    Expect Company-managed projects.
