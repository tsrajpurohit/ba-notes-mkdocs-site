**Lesson 5 -- ATS Optimization & Resume Tailoring Strategy**

**1. What is ATS?**

ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is software companies use to filter
resumes before recruiters see them.

It checks:

-   Job title match

-   Skills keywords

-   Experience relevance

-   Tools

-   Domain knowledge

**2. The Biggest Mistake**

Many candidates use **one generic resume everywhere**.

Example:

Applying for:

**Healthcare Business Analyst**

but resume says mostly:

-   Pharmacology

-   Research

-   Laboratory skills

ATS may reject because BA keywords are missing.

**4. Common BA Questions**

**Q1. What does a Business Analyst do?**

Answer:

A Business Analyst identifies business problems, understands stakeholder
needs, analyzes processes, documents requirements, and works with
technology teams to deliver solutions that achieve business objectives.

**Q2. Difference between BRD and FRD?**

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **BRD**                          **FRD**
  -------------------------------- --------------------------------------
  Business need                    System behavior

  Why build?                       What should system do?

  Business stakeholders            Business + technical teams
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

**Q3. How do you gather requirements?**

Answer:

Methods:

-   Interviews

-   Workshops

-   Observation

-   Document analysis

-   Surveys

-   Prototyping

**Q4. How do you handle changing requirements?**

Answer:

First understand the reason for change, perform impact analysis,
evaluate business value, update documentation, communicate with
stakeholders, and manage approval before implementation.

**5. Agile Questions**

**Q. What is a User Story?**

Answer:

A user story describes a requirement from the user\'s perspective,
usually written as: As a \[user\], I want \[goal\], so that \[benefit\].

Example:

As a patient, I want to book appointments online so that I can avoid
waiting time.

**Q. What is BA role in Scrum?**

BA helps with:

-   Requirement clarification

-   Backlog refinement

-   User story creation

-   Acceptance criteria

-   Stakeholder communication

-   UAT support

**6. Jira Questions**
