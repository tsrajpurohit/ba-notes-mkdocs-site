**1. What is a BA Portfolio?**

A **BA Portfolio** is a collection of your practical business analysis
work that proves:

\"I can perform BA activities in a real project.\"

**2. The First Meeting: Discovery Workshop**

A discovery workshop is the first structured conversation between:

-   Business stakeholders

-   Users

-   Product Owner

-   BA

-   Technical team (sometimes)

Purpose:

Understand WHY the organization needs a solution before discussing WHAT
to build.

**Example Project**

**AI Pharmacovigilance Platform**

Stakeholder says:

\"We need an AI system for pharmacovigilance.\"

A weak BA immediately asks:

\"What features do you need?\"

A strong BA asks:

\"Why do you need this system?\"

**Lesson 3 -- Creating Business Case & Project Charter**

**BA Professional Level**

**Learning Objective**

After this lesson, you will understand:

✅ Why every project needs a Business Case\
✅ Difference between Business Case and Project Charter\
✅ How a BA creates business justification\
✅ How to define scope, objectives, risks, assumptions\

**1. Why Does a Project Need a Business Case?**

Before an organization spends money, time, and resources, leadership
asks:

\"Why should we invest in this project?\"

A BA helps answer this question.

The Business Case proves:

-   What problem exists?

-   Why should we solve it?

-   What benefits will we get?

-   Is the investment worthwhile?

**Example**

**Business Problem**

A pharmaceutical company processes:

-   50,000 safety reports/year

-   Manual review takes 15 days

-   Compliance deadlines are difficult to maintain

The business asks:

\"Should we invest in an AI Pharmacovigilance Platform?\"

The BA creates the Business Case.

**2. Business Case vs Project Charter**

This is a common interview question.

**Business Case**

Answers:

\"WHY should we do this project?\"

Focus:

-   Problem

-   Opportunity

-   Benefits

-   Cost justification

**Project Charter**

Answers:

\"WHAT project are we starting and how will we execute it?\"

Focus:

-   Scope

-   Objectives

-   Stakeholders

-   Timeline

-   Governance

**Simple Comparison**

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Business Case**                  **Project Charter**
  ---------------------------------- ------------------------------------
  Why do we need this?               What are we going to do?

  Business justification             Project authorization

  Created before approval            Created after approval

  Focus on value                     Focus on execution
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

**1. What is a Stakeholder?**

A stakeholder is:

Any person, group, or organization that can affect the project or is
affected by the project.

A common BA mistake:

\"Only users are stakeholders.\"

Wrong.

A stakeholder can:

-   Approve the project

-   Fund the project

-   Use the system

-   Build the system

-   Validate the system

-   Reject the system

People who own the business problem.

**Lesson 14 -- UAT & Release Management**

**Validating the Product Before Business Launch**

**Learning Objective**

After this lesson, you will understand:

✅ What UAT is and why BAs manage it\
✅ Difference between QA Testing and UAT\
✅ How to create UAT plans and test scenarios\
✅ How business users validate solutions\
✅ How BAs support release management\
✅ How to document production readiness

**1. What is UAT?**

**UAT = User Acceptance Testing**

UAT answers:

\"Does this solution actually solve the business problem?\"

It is performed by:

-   Business users

-   Subject Matter Experts (SMEs)

-   Product Owner

-   Key stakeholders

**Example**

Development team says:

\"The AI extraction feature works.\"

Technical testing checks:

✅ Code works\
✅ API works\
✅ Data is stored

But business asks:

\"Can a PV Scientist actually review a safety case faster?\"

That is UAT.

**2. QA Testing vs UAT**

Common interview question.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **QA Testing**                   **UAT**
  -------------------------------- --------------------------------------
  Checks software quality          Checks business suitability

  Performed by QA team             Performed by business users

  Finds technical defects          Validates business needs

  Focus: Does it work?             Focus: Is it useful?
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

**Example:**

**QA Test**

\"Does the upload button accept PDF files?\"

**UAT Test**

\"Can PV Scientists successfully upload and process safety reports as
part of their daily workflow?\"

**3. BA Role in UAT**

The BA is responsible for:

-   Preparing UAT scenarios

-   Coordinating users

-   Explaining requirements

-   Tracking defects

-   Confirming acceptance criteria

-   Getting business sign-off
