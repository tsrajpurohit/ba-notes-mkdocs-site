**2. The STAR Framework for BA Case Studies**

A very useful interview structure.

**S --- Situation**

What was happening?

**T --- Task**

What was your responsibility?

**A --- Action**

What did you do?

**R --- Result**

What business value was achieved?

**Example:**

**Situation**

A pharmaceutical company was processing safety cases manually, resulting
in long processing cycles.

**Task**

As a BA, analyze the workflow and define requirements for an AI-assisted
solution.

**Action**

-   Conducted stakeholder interviews

-   Created AS-IS process models

-   Performed gap analysis

-   Created BRD and FRD

-   Defined user stories and acceptance criteria

**Result**

Designed a future-state workflow aimed at reducing manual effort and
improving case visibility.

↓

↓

↓

**SECTION 3: Requirement Documents**

**4. Functional Requirements Document (FRD)**

Purpose:

Explains:

What the system should do.

**Example:**

**FR-001**

Requirement:

The system shall allow patients to search doctors based on
specialization.

**Input:**

-   Medical condition

-   Location

-   Availability

**Output:**

List of matching doctors.

**5. Business Rules**

Example:

If patient selects emergency category,

then system shall recommend urgent consultation options.

**6. Traceability Matrix (RTM)**

Connects requirements to testing.

Example:

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **BR ID**              **FR ID**            **Test Case**
  ---------------------- -------------------- ----------------------------
  BR-001                 FR-001               TC-001

  BR-002                 FR-003               TC-002
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

**SECTION 4: Agile Documents**

**7. Epic**

Large business capability.

Example:

**Epic:**

AI Appointment Management

**8. Features**

**9. User Stories**

Format:

As a \<user\>

I want \<function\>

So that \<benefit\>

**Example:**

As a patient,

I want to search available doctors,

So that I can book an appointment quickly.

**Acceptance Criteria**

Given:

Patient is logged in

When:

Patient searches cardiologist

Then:

System displays available cardiologists.

**4. Common BA Interview Questions**

**Question 1:**

**How do you gather requirements?**

Strong Answer:

\"I first understand the business problem, identify stakeholders,
conduct workshops or interviews, analyze current processes, document
requirements, validate them with stakeholders, and maintain
traceability.\"

**Question 2:**

**How do you handle conflicting requirements?**

Answer:

\"I understand the reason behind each request, analyze business impact,
discuss priorities with stakeholders, document decisions, and align
requirements with business objectives.\"

**Question 3:**

**How do you prioritize requirements?**

Answer:

\"I use techniques like MoSCoW prioritization, business value analysis,
risk assessment, effort estimation, and stakeholder input.\"

**Question 4:**

**What happens if requirements change during development?**

Answer:

\"I perform impact analysis, understand affected areas, update
documentation, communicate changes, and ensure the team understands the
revised requirement.\"
