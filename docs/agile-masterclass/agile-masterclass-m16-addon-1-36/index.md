**5. RICE Prioritization Framework**

Used by Product Managers.

RICE:

R = Reach

I = Impact

C = Confidence

E = Effort

Score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) / Effort

**Example:**

**Feature:**

AI Risk Prediction

Reach:

500 users

Impact:

3 (High)

Confidence:

80%

Effort:

10 person-weeks

Calculation:

(500 × 3 × 0.8) / 10

=

120

**Feature Comparison**

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Feature**                                 **RICE Score**
  ------------------------------------------- ---------------------------
  AI Risk Prediction                          120

  Mobile App                                  40

  Chatbot                                     25
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

Decision:

Build AI Risk Prediction first.

**6. WSJF Prioritization**

Used heavily in SAFe environments.

Formula:

WSJF =

Cost of Delay

÷

Job Size

Cost of Delay:

Includes:

**Business Value**

How valuable?

**Time Criticality**

How urgent?

**Risk Reduction**

How much risk reduced?

Example:

Feature:

Regulatory Audit Trail

Business Value:

10

Time Criticality:

8

Risk Reduction:

10

Cost of Delay:

28

Job Size:

4

WSJF:

7

Higher WSJF:

Build earlier.
