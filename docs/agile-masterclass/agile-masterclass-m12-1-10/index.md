**Lesson 3: Large Language Models (LLMs)**

**1. Introduction to Large Language Models**

In previous lessons, we learned:

-   How AI changes Agile

-   AI Product Development lifecycle

-   AI Product Manager and BA roles

Now we learn the foundation behind modern AI products:

**Large Language Models (LLMs)**

**Simple Definition:**

**A Large Language Model (LLM) is an AI model trained on massive amounts
of text data to understand, generate, summarize, translate, and reason
with human language.**

In simple words:

**LLM is a very advanced language engine that learns patterns from huge
amounts of text and uses those patterns to generate useful responses.**

**2. Why Are LLMs Important?**

Before LLMs:

Software followed fixed rules.

Example:

Customer support chatbot:

User: I forgot my password

System:

If password issue →

Show reset link

Limited.

After LLMs:

User:

I cannot access my account because I changed my phone.

AI:

Understands the situation,

asks questions,

guides user through recovery.

LLMs allow:

-   Natural conversations

-   Content generation

-   Document analysis

-   Knowledge extraction

-   AI assistants

**3. Evolution of AI Language Systems**

Understanding history helps in interviews.

**Stage 1: Rule-Based Systems**

Example:

**Stage 2: Machine Learning NLP**

Models learned from examples.

**Stage 4: LLM Era**

**4. How Does an LLM Work? (Simple Explanation)**

An LLM learns language patterns.

Example:

Sentence:

\"The patient received a dose of\...\"

The model predicts:

Possible next words:

-   medication

-   treatment

-   drug

It learns:

-   Grammar

-   Context

-   Relationships between words

-   Information patterns

Basic process:

Large Text Data

↓

Training

↓

↓

↓

**5. What Does \"Large\" Mean in LLM?**

Large refers to:

**1. Training Data**

Millions/billions of documents.

Examples:

-   Books

-   Websites

-   Research papers

-   Code

-   Medical literature

**2. Parameters**

Parameters are internal values the model learns.

Example:

Small model:

Millions of parameters.

Large model:

Billions of parameters.

More parameters generally allow:

-   Better understanding

-   Better generation ability

**6. Transformer Architecture**

Important interview concept.

Modern LLMs are based on:

**Transformer Architecture**

Introduced in the paper:

\"Attention Is All You Need\"

The key innovation:

**Attention Mechanism**

Simple explanation:

Attention helps the model understand which words are important.

Example:

Sentence:

\"The patient took the drug because it reduced pain.\"

The model understands:

\"it\" refers to the drug.

Without attention:

AI may lose context.

**7. Tokens**

LLMs do not read text exactly like humans.

They process:

**Tokens**

A token is a small piece of text.

Example:

Sentence:

AI helps doctors

May become:

AI

helps

doctors

(tokens)

LLMs have token limits.

Example:

A document with thousands of pages may exceed the model\'s context
window.

**Lesson 4: Prompt Engineering**

**1. Introduction to Prompt Engineering**

In previous lessons, we learned:

-   AI Product Development

-   Large Language Models (LLMs)

-   How LLMs work

-   Healthcare AI applications

Now we learn one of the most important skills for AI Product Managers
and AI Business Analysts:

**Prompt Engineering**

**Simple Definition:**

**Prompt Engineering is the process of designing effective instructions
for AI models to generate accurate, useful, and reliable outputs.**

In simple words:

**Prompt Engineering means learning how to communicate with AI clearly
so that AI produces the desired result.**

**2. Why Prompt Engineering Matters**

An LLM does not know your exact intention.

Example:

User asks:

\"Write about drug safety.\"

AI may generate:

-   General information

-   History

-   Regulations

-   Research summary

But a pharmacovigilance expert may need:

\"Create a safety signal assessment summary.\"

Better prompt:

\"Act as a pharmacovigilance expert. Analyze this adverse event report
