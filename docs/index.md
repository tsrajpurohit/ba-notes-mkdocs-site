6\. Industry example

7\. Healthcare example

8\. AI Product example

9\. Common mistakes

10\. Best practices

11\. Interview questions

12\. Mini quiz

13\. Practical assignment

14\. Jira example

15\. Diagram (ASCII if needed)

16\. Summary

17\. Key takeaways

Additional Instructions:

\- Assume zero prior knowledge.

\- Build knowledge progressively from beginner to expert.

\- Use diagrams, flowcharts, tables, and visual representations whenever
helpful.

\- Compare concepts side by side.

\- Explain all industry jargon.

\- Include shortcuts, tips, and common interview traps.

\- Give real-world examples from companies like Amazon, Google,
Microsoft, Spotify, and healthcare organizations.

\- At the end of each module, provide:

\- Revision notes

\- Cheat sheet

\- Flashcards

\- Practical exercises

\- Portfolio tasks

\- Interview questions

\- Recommended books

\- Free learning resources

\- Track my progress and recommend what to revise based on quiz
performance.

\- Teach one lesson at a time and wait for my confirmation before moving
to the next lesson.

This prompt will produce a structured, course-like learning experience
from scratch to an advanced, interview-ready level, with a strong focus
on Business Analyst, Product Management, AI, and healthcare use cases.
