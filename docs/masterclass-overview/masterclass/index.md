**Every lesson will include**

Instead of only theory, each lesson will have:

-   **Concepts** -- What it is and why it matters.

-   **When to use it** -- Typical project situations.

-   **Step-by-step execution** -- How an experienced BA performs it.

-   **Healthcare example** -- Using pharmacovigilance, hospitals, or
    telemedicine wherever appropriate.

-   **Industry documents** -- Templates and completed examples.

-   **Common interview questions** -- What recruiters ask.

-   **Hands-on assignment** -- Practice using a realistic scenario.

-   **Common mistakes** -- Pitfalls beginners make.

-   **Professional tips** -- Techniques used by senior BAs.

**My recommendation for your learning order**

Since you\'re aiming for **Business Analyst → Product Analyst → AI
Product Manager** in healthcare, I recommend this sequence:

1.  Business Problem Analysis (Gap Analysis, RCA, Impact Analysis,
    Decision Matrix)

2.  Process Modeling (Workflow Design, BPMN)

3.  User Experience Analysis (Journey Mapping, User Flows)

4.  Solution Design (Wireframing, Prototyping)

5.  Technical BA (UML, ER Diagrams, APIs, Business Rules)

6.  Documentation (Project Charter, SRS, RTM, RAID, Change Requests,
    Test Plan, UAT, Release Notes)

7.  Agile Delivery (Advanced Scrum Board, Advanced Jira)

8.  End-to-end Industry Projects integrating all of the above

This progression mirrors how work unfolds in real projects and builds
each skill on the previous one.

Given your background in pharmacology and your goal of working on
healthcare and AI products, we\'ll use **healthcare-based case studies
throughout**, so every technique is learned in a domain that\'s directly
relevant to the roles you\'re targeting.
